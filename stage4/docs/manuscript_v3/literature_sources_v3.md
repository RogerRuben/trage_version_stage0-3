# V3 literature verification and placement

Verification date: 2026-09-12. This is a focused reference completion, not a systematic literature review. Bibliographic identity and the narrow claims used in the manuscript were checked using publisher records, author/institutional copies or standards-authority records. Some publisher full-text opens failed; no inaccessible full paper is claimed to have been comprehensively reviewed. Search snippets were not used to infer unreported experimental details.

## Four groups

| Group | Included works | Claim supported and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mixed-autonomy operations | Mo et al. 2022; Ao et al. 2024 | Existing mixed-fleet literature already addresses congestion/market interactions and strategic schedules/platform control. Do not claim all earlier models use homogeneous fleets. |
| Matching / repositioning | Alonso-Mora et al. 2017; Pavone et al. 2012 | Dynamic assignment and spatial rebalancing are established. The present unpooled capacity object is not the invention of dynamic matching. |
| Serviceability / ODD / route capability | SAE 2021; AVSC 2020; Li and Zardini 2026 | ODD terminology and operation-network/path constraints motivate conditional serviceability. Li and Zardini is explicitly a preprint, not peer-reviewed validation of our profiles. |
| Prediction / decision information | Elmachtoub and Grigas 2022; Wilder et al. 2019; Wen et al. 2019 | Prediction error and decision quality differ; transportation information timing matters. Our model is not trained with their decision-focused loss and no decision-superiority result is claimed. |
