# Main claims v3

1. A reproducible real-trajectory pipeline links road-network observations to a rolling mixed-fleet decision problem.
2. AV dispatchability is represented by hard structural feasibility plus separate continuous static, dynamic, and speed-family utilization—not by a binary safety label.
3. Nominal AV vehicle-hour share does not map one-for-one to effective dispatchable service capacity; acceptance, evidence, routing/patience, and operational envelopes intervene.
4. Frozen-state evidence shows the decision-time prediction layer is decision-relevant: P versus a Train-only historical baseline changes 9/10 assignment sets, while not claiming universal prediction superiority.
5. Family-specific cumulative exposure control has explicit guarantees and prevents cross-family compensation hidden by a scalar score.
6. Full-day fleet-transition and ODD-policy results are conditional on the frozen no-active-rebalancing baseline.
