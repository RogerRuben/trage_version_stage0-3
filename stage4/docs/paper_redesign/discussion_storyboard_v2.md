# Discussion storyboard v2

## Mechanism

The main finding is a conversion problem: nominal AV supply is progressively conditioned by passenger compatibility, operational evidence, route/capability requirements, pickup feasibility, and competition. This explains why vehicle-hour substitution need not yield equivalent dispatchable capacity.

## Representation and prediction

Separating hard feasibility from continuous family utilization retains operational attribution. Prediction matters because it changes the constrained decision environment in frozen states, not because it wins every scalar error metric.

## Policy interpretation

Gamma controls cumulative mean family exposure excess. REFERENCE is a tested compromise, not an optimized knee. Acceptance is increasingly important at higher AV penetration, but it is one gate among several.

## External validity

Results are specific to Xi'an Test31, frozen demand/supply reconstruction, capability profiles, and a no-active-rebalancing baseline. Discuss transfer as a method rather than as a numerical guarantee.

## Future work

Priorities are reproducible efficient routing, proactive rebalancing, alternative behavioral acceptance data, and prospective validation—not additional post-hoc tuning of the frozen scenarios.
